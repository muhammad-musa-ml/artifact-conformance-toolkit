# example-cache-benchmark

verdict: SUPPORTS-WITH-REVISION

Measured against `example-beta:P5-B3` on 2026-09-18, on the owner's own machine,
under the gate token the register prints beside these figures.

**The mechanism the bullet names is the mechanism this artifact measures**, and
it is a substitute rather than the example project's own system: the service the
bullet describes ran inside the firm and was never load-tested there, which the
bullet itself says. So the question this fragment answers is narrower than
"is the bullet true" -- it is "would a reader of the bullet, told these are
stand-ins, be misled by the numbers beside them".

**The direction holds and the order of magnitude holds for the tail pair.** The
cached arm serves the same offered load at a far lower tail than the uncached
arm, both arms are read at the highest rate both of them held, and both figures
sit in the same band the bullet's pair sits in. A reader who took the bullet to
mean "fronting this lookup with a read-through cache cuts the tail by roughly
this much" would not be misled about the direction or about the size of the
effect.

**Both of the bullet's figures nevertheless have to change, and that is why
this is a revision rather than a confirmation.** The measured tail pair is
lower on both arms than the pair the bullet quotes, so the bullet's absolute
numbers are not the ones this machine produces. That is a straightforward
refresh of two values.

**The capacity claim is the harder half, and it is refuted as a point rather
than merely moved.** The bullet quotes a single capacity ratio. This campaign's
open-loop ladder steps coarsely, so each arm's capacity figure is a quantised
LOWER BOUND -- the arm held its highest passing level and failed at the next
one -- and a quotient of two lower bounds is a function of the ladder's step
size, not of capacity. The derivation refuses to author such a ratio and this
artifact publishes a bracket per arm instead. The bullet's figure lies outside
the interval those brackets allow, so it cannot be kept, and no replacement
point figure can honestly be offered from this campaign. The revision the
bullet needs here is a change of KIND: a bounded statement where it currently
carries a point.

**What the bullet may keep** is the mechanism, the direction, the stand-in
disclosure it already carries non-trimmably, and the tail pair once its numbers
are refreshed. What it may not keep is a single capacity ratio.

**This verdict is the OWNER's reading and is recorded here for confirmation at
the close of this work, not asserted.** "Similar" is bounded by the bullet's own
basis, and whether a reader would be misled is a judgment rather than a
computation; the checker verifies that a verdict exists and is legal and never
that it is right. Every number behind this reading is generated beside this
fragment in the register, and none of it is retyped here.
